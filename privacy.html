Privacy notice

Read the Terms and Conditions.

When you complete the DataReadyTest.online practice assessment, the site collects the name and email address you enter, your selected judgments and written explanations, and the date and time the report is submitted. This is an independent practice assessment only; it is not an official company or work assessment and does not produce an accuracy score, certification, or hiring decision.

Your completed report is sent to the website server and stored in a private server directory. It is visible to the site administrator through a password-protected page at /admin.php. Reports remain stored until the administrator deletes them. If you do not want your report stored, do not continue with the assessment.

The hosting provider processes requests needed to operate the site. This page does not currently include an advertising network. If an ad provider is added later, this notice must be updated to explain that provider's cookies, data practices, and any required choices before ads are enabled.

For questions about a stored report or to request its deletion, contact the site administrator using the contact details provided by the site owner.
